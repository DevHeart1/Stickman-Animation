# Stickman Animation

Research and production notes for short-form vertical stickman comedy skits.

- **[research/viral-stickman-tiktok-analysis.md](research/viral-stickman-tiktok-analysis.md)** is a frame-by-frame breakdown of six viral stickman TikTok skits. It covers full transcripts, hooks, joke structures, shot lists, camera moves, animation cadence, lighting and colour measurements, caption specs, sound mix, and a production blueprint for making original skits in this style.
- `research/frames/` holds contact sheets (including full 30 fps sheets of hooks and punchlines), stills and spectrograms.
- `research/data/` holds per-frame metrics (30 fps CSV), per-line audio analysis and shot summaries.
- `research/tools/` holds the scripts used to produce the analysis.
